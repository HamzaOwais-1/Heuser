Stat Arena

My /api page uses PokeAPI to build a tug-of-war between two Pokémon. Visitors search for any two fighters, and each of their six base stats becomes a bar that pulls on a rope. Whoever wins a stat drags the knot toward their side, and the fighter who holds the knot after six rounds is the champion. It is unique because the numbers aren't shown as a table or list: they become a physical contest you watch and replay.
